# Ustad: 3-minute pitch script and prototype-video shot list

## The 3-minute pitch (one speaker, ~420 words)

| Time | Slide | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Cover | “Hi, we’re [team]. This is Ustad — the master technician in your glasses.” |
| 0:10 | Problem | “Every workshop in the UAE has one senior who knows everything, and many juniors who guess. They replace parts that were fine, jobs wait for the senior, and when seniors leave, their knowledge leaves with them.” |
| 0:30 | Meet Ustad | “Ustad puts that senior inside Meta glasses. It does three things: inspect, explain, teach.” |
| 0:40 | How it works | “A technician opens the bonnet and asks: ‘Ustad, it won’t start, what’s happening here?’ The glasses take a photo; faces and plates are blurred on the phone. Ustad ranks the causes — corroded battery terminals, 71% — and coaches the fix step by step, checking the result by photo.” |
| 1:05 | The brain | “It isn’t just an AI guess. The AI only reports what it sees. Ustad’s own knowledge graph does the diagnosis, explains which evidence pointed where, and learns from every fix a technician confirms.” |
| 1:20 | See inside | “Then it shows the hidden part in 3D — and on Ray-Ban Display glasses, right in the lens. Voice in English, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam and Tagalog.” |
| 1:35 | Tutor | “And here’s what no one else does: an expert does a job once, Ustad turns it into a lesson in their own words, and every new learner is coached through it and earns a Skill Passport.” |
| 1:55 | Trust | “Privacy is the order of operations: capture on command, blur on device, send the minimum, log every request in the app, keep keys on our server, and stop dangerous jobs with fixed safety rules.” |
| 2:15 | Proof | “It’s a working Android app on Meta’s Device Access Toolkit, 83 automated tests, and our brain ranked 9 out of 9 public fault photos correctly. [Our own engine-bay test: __%.]” |
| 2:30 | Market | “Remote-assist glasses connect you to an expert. Ustad becomes the expert — on consumer Meta glasses.” |
| 2:40 | Model + next | “We sell per technician to workshops, training centres and brands. In 90 days: a 30-photo field test, live look mode, and a paid pilot with UAE garages.” |
| 2:55 | Close | “One expert’s knowledge, in every technician’s glasses. Thank you.” |

## The real prototype video (about 2 minutes)

**Set-up:** Ustad v2.7 debug build on the phone · Mock Device Kit enabled, paired, Powered and Donned, camera = phone back camera · a real car with the bonnet closed (ideally a battery terminal with some white crust) · a second phone to film the person · quiet place · phone screen recording ON.

**Mount:** strap the Ustad phone to the chest (or a head strap) so its camera sees what the person sees. That phone is the “glasses camera” through the Mock Device Kit.

| # | Time | Shot (second phone) | Screen (Ustad phone) | Action / words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | Wide: person walks to the car | Glasses screen, session started | Title card: “Ustad — real prototype, Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit (Mock Device Kit)” |
| 2 | 0:08–0:18 | Medium: opens the bonnet | — | Opens the bonnet, leans in |
| 3 | 0:18–0:28 | Close on face | Wake word chip | “Hey Mentor, inspect.” (your wake word) — then “It won’t start, what’s happening here?” |
| 4 | 0:28–0:45 | Over the shoulder | Capture → “Ustad is looking…” → result | Hold still for the photo; show the 🔒 blurred note |
| 5 | 0:45–1:05 | — (screen only) | Result: causes with %, Inside view, tap Explode | Rotate the 3D car with a finger |
| 6 | 1:05–1:15 | — | More details → How Ustad decided | Say “yes” / “no” to Ustad’s question |
| 7 | 1:15–1:35 | Hands at the battery | Repair coach | “Next”, “why” — Ustad answers by voice |
| 8 | 1:35–1:45 | Close: phone camera on terminal | Prove it → 📸 → confirmed | “Hey Mentor, check.” |
| 9 | 1:45–1:55 | — | Learn tab → built-in lesson → Skill Passport | Quick scroll |
| 10 | 1:55–2:05 | — | Settings → What left this phone | Show the log: blurred photo + text only |
| 11 | 2:05–2:10 | — | — | End card: “Ustad. One expert’s knowledge, in every technician’s glasses.” |

**Tips:** record each shot 2–3 times; keep the phone volume up so Ustad’s voice is in the recording; if the network is slow, use the built-in lesson (no AI needed) for shots 7–9. Send the clips and screen recordings and they can be cut together with titles and the CGI film as a 15-second opener.
